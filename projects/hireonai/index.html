A career product that matches a résumé to real jobs, explains the score, names the gaps, and drafts the application — without inventing experience the candidate does not have.

The loop.

1. Read the résumé as evidence

Skills and roles are treated as claims to check against a job, not as keywords to inflate.

2. Score with a reason

A match score without a reason is theatre. HireOnAI has to say why the number moved.

3. Name the gaps

Missing experience is listed. The product does not paper over it with confident prose.

4. Draft, don’t invent

Application text can be tailored. It cannot invent a company, a title, or a skill that is not on the page.
