Technical Decisions

Why Qwen3-4B?

Strong instruction-following at 4B params — small enough to quantize to 4-bit and train on a free T4 (15 GB VRAM), capable enough for coherent domain output.

Why LoRA?

Frozen base weights + trainable low-rank adapters. Result: a 120 MB artifact instead of a 2 GB model, faster training, and dramatically lower hardware requirements.

Why Unsloth?

Custom Triton kernels deliver ~2× faster training with lower VRAM usage — the difference between fitting on free-tier Colab and not.

Why 4-bit Quantization?

load_in_4bit (NF4) shrinks memory footprint ~4× with negligible quality loss, making consumer-GPU fine-tuning viable.

Generalization Proof

Evaluated on a scenario absent from training data (fitness-studio competitive strategy). The model produced coherent, differentiated strategic advice — evidence of learned behavior, not memorization.

Reproducibility

The full model reloads from huggingface.co alone — no local checkpoints. The Hub URL is the single source of truth for the entire artifact.

Reproduce It in Two Lines

from unsloth import FastLanguageModel

# Load the fine-tuned model straight from the Hugging Face Hub
model, tokenizer = FastLanguageModel.from_pretrained(
    model_name="maxrajiv-buda/ira-dolphin",
    max_seq_length=2048
    max_seq_length=2048,
    load_in_4bit=True,
)
FastLanguageModel.for_inference(model)

# Ask it a novel business question
messages = [{"role": "user", "content": "Give strategic advice for a small fitness studio facing competition from a big gym chain."}]
text = tokenizer.apply_chat_template(messages, tokenize=False, add_generation_prompt=True)
inputs = tokenizer(text, return_tensors="pt").to("cuda")
out = model.generate(**inputs, max_new_tokens=200)
print(tokenizer.decode(out[0], skip_special_tokens=True))
